Join or renew

The club is now affiliated with the French Tennis Federation (FFT). In practice: your membership goes through an FFT licence, and everything is done online from home. Allow ten minutes.

Register on Ten'Up

The procedure

Four steps, in this order

The steps depend on each other: no Ten'Up account means no licence number, and no licence means no valid membership.

Note for English speakers: Ten'Up and HelloAsso are French services and their pages are in French only. If that is a problem, skip straight to the last section below — we will register you ourselves.

  1. Create your Ten'Up account

    Every member needs their own account, in their own name — including within the same family. That account carries your FFT licence number. A shared account will not work.

    Create an account on tenup.fft.fr

  2. Choose the club's offer

    Go to the Pickleball Vexin Centre offers page and pick the one that applies to you.

    See the club offers on Ten'Up

  3. Pay through HelloAsso

    Payment happens on HelloAsso at the end of the process. Remember to set the voluntary contribution back to €0: it is added by default to support HelloAsso, and it will be charged on top of your membership fee if you leave it as it is.

  4. Collect your licence number

    Once payment clears, your licence appears in your Ten'Up account. It covers you for insurance, and it is required to enter sanctioned tournaments.

Stuck?

We can do it for you

If creating the Ten'Up account or paying through HelloAsso is blocking you, do not stay stuck: write to us and we will handle the registration with you.
