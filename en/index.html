You said pickleball?

What it actually is

A mix of tennis, badminton and table tennis, played mostly as doubles on a badminton-sized court. The ball is perforated plastic, the paddle is solid, and the pace sets itself: you can rally gently or hit very hard.

  • The rules take ten minutes to learn. Serves are underhand, struck below the waist.
  • The non-volley zone — the “kitchen” — stops you smashing at the net. That is what makes rallies long.
  • Little running, a lot of positioning. The court is 13.4 m by 6.1 m, net included.

First time

Come and try

Let us know before you drop by — we will meet you courtside and lend you a paddle. Clean indoor shoes with light soles, a bottle of water, that is all.
