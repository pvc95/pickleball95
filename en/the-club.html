The club

A village club, three gyms

Pickleball Vexin Centre puts complete beginners and tournament players on the same courts, on the same evenings. That is rather the point of pickleball: levels mix without anyone getting bored.

Federation Affiliated with the French Tennis Federation (FFT)
Licence Through Ten'Up, individual account required
Area Vexin français, Val-d'Oise (95), north-west of Paris
Sessions Wednesday in Vigny, Friday in Chars, Sunday in Marines

What's new

What FFT affiliation changes

From this season, the club's pickleball is attached to the FFT. Three concrete consequences for you:

  • Registration happens online, on Ten'Up, with an account in your own name.
  • You get a licence number, and the insurance that comes with it.
  • Sanctioned tournaments are open to you, and the club can host them.
