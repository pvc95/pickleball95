FFT-sanctioned tournament · Sunday 4 October 2026

1st K100 in Cergy

Pickleball Vexin Centre is hosting its first sanctioned tournament, at the Val-d'Oise tennis committee. One day, two doubles events, two levels of draw. Registration is open.

How it works

Registering, in three steps

  1. Create your DUPR ID

    It is asked for at registration. If you do not have one yet, creating it is free and takes two minutes.

    Create a DUPR account

  2. Pick your event

    FFT rules forbid entering two draws played on the same day. Men must therefore choose between men's doubles and mixed doubles, not both.

  3. Register on HelloAsso

    Select the ticket matching your event and level, then pay the €20 entry fee. HelloAsso will then offer to add a donation: it is optional, choose “Pas de don” if you would rather not contribute.
