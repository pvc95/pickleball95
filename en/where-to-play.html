From Cergy-Pontoise and around

How far it is

The three gyms sit in the Vexin français, north-west of the Paris region. From central Cergy or Pontoise it is 12 to 16 kilometres, about twenty minutes by car on the D14 or the D915. Paris itself is around 40 kilometres away.

Players come to us from Cergy, Pontoise, Osny, Meulan-en-Yvelines, Magny-en-Vexin and the surrounding villages. There is no closer pickleball club in the Vexin, and all three sessions are open to every member whichever gym you start at.

  • Vigny is the easiest to reach from Cergy-Pontoise and Meulan.
  • Chars is closest if you come from Magny-en-Vexin or the Oise.
  • Marines sits halfway, handy on Sundays from anywhere in the area.

Free parking is available at each gym.

Good to know

Before you come

  • Clean indoor shoes with light soles are required in all three gyms.
  • Paddles and balls are lent for your first sessions. The club provides shared equipment.
  • No sessions during French school holidays or public holidays, unless we announce otherwise on social media.
  • Last-minute changes (gym closed, session moved) are announced on Facebook and Instagram.
